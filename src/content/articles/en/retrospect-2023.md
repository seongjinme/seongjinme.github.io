## Someone who cares

Two years ago, learning was my job. These days, when people ask what I do, I say I am a homemaker. Disliking the old-fashioned origins of the Korean word, I once tried calling myself a domestic worker. But people kept asking what that meant, so now I just say homemaker.

Housework and care have become the largest part of my life because of this little one, Dooboo.

![Dooboo out for a walk, November 10, 2023](/content/images/2023/12/dooboo.jpg)

Rescued last April after being tied up in an unknown field. As unruly as a child, and far cleverer than one. I took Dooboo in temporarily, hoping to send this dog safely into a world free of prejudice about being large and fierce. Now I live with the intention of providing that world myself. Every day is full of lively, unpredictable incidents. We have been together only a few months, but it is already hard to imagine life without this little one.

Choices come at a price. Housework that once meant looking after my partner and myself snowballed. Raising a living being who will spend a lifetime with the intelligence of a three-year-old, without a shared language, was no small task. Walks, toilet breaks, hygiene, meals, socializing with people and dogs, manners classes, guardian training, play, and occasional medical checkups consumed hundreds of minutes in scattered pieces every day. Even after all that time, I kept blaming myself for falling short as a guardian.

I have heard so much about work-life balance. Looking back, it was all nonsense. In the world of housework and care, work and life are just one mass. A pile of tangled events, layer upon layer. To clear the new layers that appear without warning every day, all I can do is chop up the time I have and somehow divide and conquer. That is how I get through a day.

## Someone who learns

The days when I could spend a dozen hours a day absorbed in work or study are over. Now I balance care and learning, managing time by the minute. To gather those short fragments and accomplish as much as others, I had to concentrate several times harder. Learning this year was a continuous struggle.

- [Earned the HarvardX Professional Certificate in Computer Science for Web Programming](https://www.edx.org/certificates/professional-certificate/harvardx-computer-science-for-web-programming) (January-April)
- Graduated from Korea National Open University's Computer Science department (February)
- [Built and deployed a quiz app using the ChatGPT API](https://kinoquiz.app/) (April)
- Entered an LLM app competition: developed an AI solution for generating news monitoring reports and answering questions (August-September)
- [Took the sixth Woowacourse frontend precourse](https://www.woowacourse.io/) (October-December)
- [Took the Programmers Web Full-Cycle Development Devcourse](https://school.programmers.co.kr/learn/courses/19259/19259-kdt-%ED%83%80%EC%9E%85%EC%8A%A4%ED%81%AC%EB%A6%BD%ED%8A%B8%EB%A1%9C-%ED%95%A8%EA%BB%98%ED%95%98%EB%8A%94-%EC%9B%B9-%ED%92%80-%EC%82%AC%EC%9D%B4%ED%81%B4-%EA%B0%9C%EB%B0%9Creact-nodejs-1%EA%B8%B0) (November-December)
- [Renewed my GCP Associate Cloud Engineer certification](https://cloud.google.com/learn/certification/cloud-engineer) (December)

No account of this year's technology trends could leave out the popularization of GPT and other large language models (LLMs). In a world swept by generative AI, I did not want to remain someone dragged beneath the waves. Nor did I want to be swayed by people suddenly posing as gurus of a new era, like vendors chasing a seasonal trade. I wanted to learn to ride those waves myself, however feebly. Studying prompt engineering, building a ChatGPT API quiz app, and entering an LLM app competition were all small efforts to get over the swell of the times.

- [Prompt engineering for better results from ChatGPT and other conversational AI services](/en/prompt-engineering-in-chatgpt/)
- [Building a web application through ChatGPT conversation: a prompting example and its implications](/en/experiment-of-creating-web-app-with-chatgpt/)
- [Building KinoQuizAI with the OpenAI API as a backend data agent](/en/using-openai-api-as-a-content-generator/)

Building the AI quiz app made me feel keenly that even a service gilded with cutting-edge technology would struggle to succeed without an appealing user experience. That sparked a serious interest in designing and implementing the point where a service meets its users. I joined the Woowacourse precourse and the Programmers full-stack Devcourse in the fourth quarter to learn JavaScript, the core language of web frontend technology. Around then, my goal changed from "cloud engineer" to "web developer who can also work with the cloud."

## Someone who trains

To endure the upheaval in daily life and hold my unsettled mind steady, I needed to make my body a stronger vessel. For years I have trained with kettlebells under [instructor Hyun Jin Choi](https://www.strongfirst.com/instructors/korea,-republic-of/hyun-jin-choi.001370000076Y5qAAE/), imagining that I was covering ruins with soil, leveling the ground, and laying new foundations.

Last October, I earned StrongFirst's SFG Level 1 instructor certification. It had been two years and six months since my first-ever pull-up.

![SFG Level 1 certificate, October 22, 2023](/content/images/2023/12/sfg.jpg)

For someone who has never thought of teaching as a profession, the words "instructor certification" feel heavy. I would rather see it as a small marker of steady practice. Even someone who spent more than thirty years regarding exercise as a distant concern can do this by working consistently. I am satisfied to have proved that.

## Someone moving forward, a little at a time

In February 2024, I will be a student again. I will spend next year as **a frontend student in the sixth cohort of [Woowacourse](https://www.woowacourse.io/)**.

Originally, I applied just for the precourse experience. I wanted to improve my ability to use JavaScript and its superset languages across web frontend, backend, and cloud infrastructure services. I had done no special preparation. I only intended to spend four weeks stumbling through unfamiliar problems and learning as much as I could. My application briefly described what I had felt while studying, writing, and making things. With rather more confidence than was warranted, I submitted only my GitHub profile as supporting material.

The precourse changed me more than I expected. Watching thousands of people discuss the same problem through code and share their knowledge every week was a shock. [As a beginner, all I did was work the knowledge and feedback I picked up there into each week's assignment.](/en/woowacourse-three-design-patterns-in-javascript/) Code I had spent an entire week building with all my strength became an ugly relic of a former life by the next week. Again and again.

I had never improved so much in so little time. Probably because I knew so little to begin with. That makes me curious about the main course. It will be busier and more demanding. Painful sometimes. Still, I will go in with the easygoing attitude that I only need to move forward as much as I can, a little at a time, and that it is all right if I cannot. Constantly measuring the gap between myself and others and driving myself harder would only end in self-abuse.

## And,

This was a year of trying not to let go of care, learning, or exercise. It was possible only because of my beloved partner, our adorable new family member, and the people who have helped me. Their generous encouragement steadied me whenever worry about an uncertain future threatened to leave me feeling utterly alone. [As I have said before, all my courage came from them.](/en/retrospect-2021/#and-what-i-realized)
